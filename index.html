FEEDWISE

Snap a plant. Know what to feed it.

Feedwise is an iPhone app that looks at a photo of your plant, lawn or crop and tells you which fertilizer ratio (N-P-K) to use, how much for your pots, garden or acreage, and when to apply it based on local soil and the weather forecast.

How it works

  • Take a photo of the whole plant or a close-up of its leaves, in good light.
  • Tell us the size: pot width and count, garden square feet, or acres.
  • Get your recommendation: the N-P-K ratio, the amount, the best timing, and matching products from several brands and stores.

Common questions

How accurate is it? Feedwise combines what's visible in the photo with typical crop needs, local soil survey data and the forecast. A photo can't measure soil nutrients, so treat results as guidance, always follow the product label, and for large plantings confirm with a soil test from your county extension office.

Does Feedwise sell fertilizer? No. We point you to products from several brands and stores, and we aren't paid by them.

How many free scans do I get? Three. After that, Feedwise Pro gives you unlimited scans (fair use: up to 40 a day).

How do I cancel my subscription? On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Feedwise. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

I subscribed but the app still asks me to pay. Open Settings in Feedwise and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
